Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026
In short: everything you enter in Fiala stays on your own device. Fiala has no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nobody else, including the people who make Fiala, can see your information.

What Fiala stores, and where

Fiala saves the information you choose to enter so the app can work. It is stored only in your device's local storage for the app (or, on the website, your browser's storage for the Fiala site).

InformationWhy
Doses you log (compound name, amount, time, injection site, notes)Your dose history and the Today screen
Vials you record (amount, water added, date mixed, notes)Showing what is left and when to discard
Schedules you create (compound, amount, days, time)The Today screen and reminders
Whether you finished the welcome walkthrough, and when you last made a backupNot showing the walkthrough twice; backup reminders

Fiala does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information. It does not read data from Apple Health, Google Health Connect or other apps.

Health information

What you log can reveal information about your health. Fiala treats all of it as sensitive: it never leaves your device unless you export it yourself, and it is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

Notifications

If you turn on dose reminders in the iPhone or Android app, Fiala schedules notifications on your device using your phone's own notification system. Reminder content (such as the compound name and amount) is created on your device and is not sent anywhere. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's Settings.

On the website, "Add to calendar" creates a calendar file on your device. If you add it to a calendar account (for example iCloud or Google Calendar), that calendar provider's privacy policy applies to it.

Backups and exports

Fiala can create a backup file or a CSV export of your log. These files are made on your device and saved wherever you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email). Once you save or send a file somewhere, that service's privacy policy applies. Backups contain your health records, so keep them somewhere private.

Sharing and selling

Fiala does not share, sell or rent your information to anyone, because it never receives it.

Website hosting

The Fiala website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads the site, under GitHub's privacy statement. Fiala itself adds no tracking, cookies or third-party scripts.

News feed

To show the News section, the website and the iPhone and Android apps download a public list of headlines from getfiala.com (hosted on GitHub Pages, as above) a few times a day while you use the app. The download is the same for everyone: it contains nothing about you, and your log, schedules and the peptides you follow are never sent. Which peptides you follow is stored only on your device. Tapping a headline opens the original source, such as PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov or the FDA website, whose own privacy policy then applies. Apart from the news feed, the iPhone and Android apps load nothing from the internet.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the app at any time. To delete everything:

  • iPhone or Android app: delete the app.
  • Website: clear the site data for Fiala in your browser settings, or remove the home-screen app and then clear the site data.

Because Fiala has no copy of your data, deleted data cannot be recovered unless you kept a backup file.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws give you rights over personal data an organisation holds about you. Fiala holds none: your data is on your device and under your control, so you can view, export, correct or delete it yourself at any time.

Children

Fiala is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Fiala is for education and personal record-keeping only. It is not medical advice and does not recommend doses. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional before using any peptide.

Changes to this policy

If Fiala starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated before the change and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@getfiala.com or see the Fiala support page.
